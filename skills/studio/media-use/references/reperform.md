# Re-performance: putting a person into an existing video

To put the person into an existing video (a performance, a dance, a talk), name that project video as reference_video (and reference_start, the second to begin at), a few seconds of video of the person looking at the camera as face_clip (best: four frames are taken from it; or three or four photos as references, all from one sitting, which are refused if they don't agree as one face; ask the person for such a clip or their HeyGen avatar's video), their look in words as person (with more than one person in the video the plan shows each one's face and asks which to replace: call again with performer set to that letter), in prompt what the shot shows (the set, the clothing), and delivery sings for a song. It is re-performed as the best measured run did it: pieces that never cross the source's cuts, each from the original with every face and its hair blurred and with its own stretch of the original sound, one take per piece and more (up to `takes`, default 4) only for a piece whose take doesn't follow the original, the take whose head tracks the original closest kept, never one that shows the original face (a piece where every take does gets one wider-blurred round). The first call only plans (don't ask the person to approve it; go on): it returns each piece's frame sheet and the clip's audio; Read the sheets, transcribe the audio, and call again with reperform_job, notes {piece: {moves}} (the head, eyes, expression and body movements you see, with times) and words. A minute is about 25 pieces and more than one call: while it says processing, call again with the same reperform_job; nothing paid for is made again. The body (arms, build) can still come from the original.
